# Version 1.1 verification — 2026-10-25

The expansion adds 63 destinations to the original 36: **99 countries**, with **32 in Africa**, **28 in Europe**, 24 in Asia, 13 in the Americas and 2 in Oceania. Existing numeric country IDs, the manifest identity and the `bar-world-v1` save key remain stable.

- **15 automated tests pass.** Coverage includes all 99 bilingual country records and silhouettes; all 1,368 narration/greeting files; randomized valid journeys; original save recovery; animal distractors with shared species, including camel aliases; map-ID regressions; and non-overlapping nearby-country quiz markers.
- **113 browser assertions pass.** Every game completes an eight-question journey; mistakes and repeated taps preserve reward integrity; exact questions resume; every recording decodes; install metadata is present; a real waiting update activates safely and reloads at the next question; settings, stars and active work survive; and offline launch/art/audio work without runtime exceptions.
- **141 original-to-expanded release assertions pass.** A real cached copy of the original 36-country release is seeded with a partly completed journey, old stamps and custom settings. The expansion waits during the active question and activates at a safe navigation. The next original question, stars, stamps, language, volume and motion preferences survive. Unrelated games’ local storage and caches remain intact. Every one of the 63 new cards opens with its Hebrew name and final artwork and fits a 1024×768 tablet. Long English names and new offline artwork/greetings also pass.
- **20 long-prompt layouts pass.** All five games fit 1024×768 landscape and 768×1024 portrait tablets in Hebrew and English, including the Democratic Republic of the Congo and long animal names. Bar’s portrait is smaller on short tablets to keep controls in view; animal pictures preserve the full scene height so tall animals’ heads remain visible.
- **8 real pointer-answer regressions pass.** Both globe activities work with the tightly clustered Lithuania/Latvia/Estonia/Belarus choices. Quiz markers separate with leader lines while preserving their true geographic anchors. Every marker can actually be tapped without another marker intercepting the answer.

Original-release update compatibility: the old release may wait until a return to the globe or another safe navigation before applying a downloaded expansion. The expanded release explicitly identifies the saved next-question boundary, so future updates also apply there. Neither path resets or replaces an unfinished journey. Update checks remain startup-only; no timer, focus or reconnection checks were added.

Seven new nine-scene atlases and all 63 extracted scenes were visually reviewed. The eleven retained atlases are indexed by ISO code in `art-source/atlas-catalog.json`. The original country artwork remains unchanged. The Congo church is labelled generically after art review rather than attributing simplified generated architecture to a specific basilica.

Reports and screenshots are in ignored `artifacts/`. Reproduce with `npm test`, `scripts/browser-check.mjs`, `scripts/check-expansion-upgrade.mjs`, `scripts/check-question-layouts.mjs`, `scripts/check-quiz-markers.mjs` and `scripts/check-live.mjs`. The legacy upgrade check needs a built original checkout (commit `be9a456`) at `artifacts/original-release/`.

Physical Android/iPad OS installation and playback through actual device speakers were not tested here. Browser-specific installation and OS storage/audio behavior depend on the device. Synthetic native-language recordings still benefit from human pronunciation review.
